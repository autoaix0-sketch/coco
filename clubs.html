Quick answers

Which Barcelona clubs are free tonight?

KU and Jamboree are free every night. Pick a night on the guest list page to see every club free that night.

What are the best beach clubs in Barcelona?

KU, Opium, Shôko and CDLC, side by side in Port Olímpic on the Barceloneta seafront.

What should I wear?

Smart casual. Clean sneakers are fine; save the sportswear, flip-flops and beach shorts for the day.
