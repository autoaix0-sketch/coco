    Good to know

    Is it really free?

    Yes. Joining and entry are free. Drinks inside are at the bar's prices.

    What should I wear?

    Smart casual. Clean sneakers are fine; save the sportswear, flip-flops and beach shorts for the day.

    When should I arrive?

    12:30–12:45 is the sweet spot. Free entry runs until 1:00, and until 1:30 at Otto Zutz and Twenties.

    Coming with friends?

    Do I need ID?

    Yes, clubs are 18+. Bring your passport or ID card.
